Frequently asked questions

How do I submit an assignment?

Open the course, find the assignment, click Submit, upload your file and confirm.

Why can't I open a quiz?

Some quizzes are restricted to certain networks, or are not open yet. Connect to the approved network or ask your lecturer.

I forgot my password

Ask an administrator to reset it. You will get a temporary password and be asked to choose your own at first sign-in.

How do I contact my lecturer?

Use Messages, or the course's forum.

How do I get my certificate?

When you meet a course's completion rules, the certificate appears under Certificates and can be downloaded as a PDF.
